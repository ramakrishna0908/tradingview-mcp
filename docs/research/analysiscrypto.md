## Methodology & Data Sourcing

- **Technicals** (trend, RSI, MACD-proxy, support/resistance, money flow) are pulled **live from TradingView** (daily timeframe, Coinbase/aggregated spot) via the CDP bridge. Indicator suite on chart: Bollinger Bands, EMA, Q2-2026 Anchored VWAP, Ichimoku Cloud, RSI (+ signal MA), Chaikin Money Flow (CMF), ATR. *(A dedicated MACD study is loaded but the primary momentum read here uses RSI-vs-its-MA + price-vs-cloud/EMA, which is equivalent for trend/momentum direction; noted per token.)*
- **Fundamentals** (tokenomics, supply/unlocks, TVL, protocol revenue, adoption, dev activity) are gathered via **web research against primary aggregators** — CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, DefiLlama, Messari, Token Terminal, Tokenomist, project docs/blogs. **Data-as-of dates are cited**; fast-moving figures are approximate and flagged where sources conflict.
- **Honesty rules:** no fabricated metrics. Where current data was not found, it is marked *"no current data found."* Early-stage tokens (esp. ZG/0G) carry wide data-uncertainty and are flagged accordingly.

### Conviction Score (/100) — how it's built
Five weighted pillars, scored 0–100 then blended:

| Pillar | Weight | What it measures |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Fundamentals & Moat** | 30% | Real product-market fit, defensibility, competitive position |
| **Tokenomics** | 25% | Supply discipline, inflation vs burns, unlock overhang, staking sink, value accrual |
| **Adoption & Revenue** | 20% | Usage, TVL/revenue, dev activity, growth trajectory |
| **Catalysts vs Risks** | 15% | Net of upcoming drivers minus structural risks |
| **Technical Setup** | 10% | Trend/momentum/flow — timing overlay, not the thesis |

**Recommendation bands:** Strong Buy 80–100 · Buy 70–79 · Accumulate 60–69 · Hold 45–59 · Reduce 30–44 · Avoid <30.
*(Technicals are a 10% timing overlay only — a strong long-term thesis with a weak chart = "Accumulate on weakness," not "Avoid.")*
